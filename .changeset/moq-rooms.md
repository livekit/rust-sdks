Add opt-in native MoQ room transport for Opus audio, H.264, H.265, VP8, VP9 and AV1 video, and per-track lossy datagrams using LiveKit MoQ profile 2 on the SDK and SFU. Rooms can keep their publications and subscriptions across network route changes and receive first-subscriber, track failure, speaker and connection quality events over LiveKit signaling.
